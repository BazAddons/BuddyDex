## Joshua: complete direct referrals and chain follow-through

Read the full objective, description and all supplied progress/completion text for each Classic quest below. The installed quest corpus corroborates matching offer/completion for Returning Home and the four Cenarion steps; Arcane Feedback has completion only in that corpus.

- [Returning Home 5631](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5631): Joshua passes on a visiting priestess's summons to a night elf priest and directs them to Priestess Alathea at the Temple of the Moon in Darnassus. Alathea's completion welcomes the pupil home, mentions Tyrande, asks about Elune's blessing and wants to hear about their travels. Those are Alathea's words and relationship, not proof Joshua trained the pupil alongside Tyrande. The quest ends at the referral in these sources; no spell reward or present racial restriction is promised. Other trainers' same-title referrals are alternatives.
- [Arcane Feedback 5676](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5676): the source associates this offer with human Joshua and a human pupil, but the description addresses the pupil as another race and expresses mistrust of arcane magic. That race contrast is inconsistent with the identified giver/pupil combination. Completion belongs to High Priestess Laurena and concerns a protective spell. **The ambiguous offer is omitted from Joshua's personality and profile facts.** It does not turn him into a night elf or prove an individual prejudice. The installed completion alone cannot resolve the offer attribution.
- [Cenarion Aid 8254](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=8254): a shared trainer referral asks priests to help the Cenarion Circle through Ogtinc in Azshara. The reason for aid is the need, beyond differences in approach. Ogtinc welcomes the arrival. Joshua is one of the listed givers, not the creator of the entire operation.
- [Of Coursers We Know 8255](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=8255): Ogtinc seeks courser glands for a restorative salve. Completion explains why the Circle finds killing these sacred animals spiritually troubling and values the outsider's help. This is Ogtinc's explanation, not Joshua personally hunting them.
- [The Ichor of Undeath 8256](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=8256): Ogtinc reluctantly needs a potent undead ingredient as part of the salve. His distaste and gratitude remain his. The profile imports no ingredient formula or invented position on all undead people.
- [Blood of Morphaz 8257](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=8257): Ogtinc sends the player and allies to the Sunken Temple for the green drake's blood, delivering it to Greta Mosshoof in Felwood. Completion begins restorative work and says recovery will take a lifetime. It does not establish that Felwood is already healed, that Joshua visited the temple, or that the current player has completed the chain.

Alathea's Classic start/end lists were checked to distinguish the same-title referrals from unrelated later lessons. The [Forever Cenarion reference](https://urgametips.com/wow-forever/quest-guides/cenarion-aid-priest-trainers-ogtinc-azshara/) corroborates Joshua-to-Ogtinc and the four-step chain, while clearly remaining external evidence rather than a live quest window.
