[Farmountain](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thief_Catcher_Farmountain), [Shadowdelve](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thief_Catcher_Shadowdelve), and [Thunderbrew](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Thief_Catcher_Thunderbrew) are male dwarves. Hundreds of database map points are not hundreds of distinct people with those names. Later retail level increases and model changes are excluded; the local level-60 sighting and Classic models are used.
