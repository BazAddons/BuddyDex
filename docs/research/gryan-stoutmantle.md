# Gryan Stoutmantle: source and continuity notes

Researched November 8, 2026. NPC **234**, human male paladin, leader of the People's Militia at Sentinel Hill, Westfall. No live dialogue or rendered layout was inspected by the agent.

## Militia and return home

[The People's Militia chain](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_People%27s_Militia_quest_chain) progresses through three campaigns against Defias groups. [Part 2](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_People%27s_Militia_%282%29) emphasizes protecting local peace and prosperity. [Part 3's completion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_People%27s_Militia_%283%29) explicitly recalls his return from Lordaeron to his homeland and his hope for Westfall. This supplies the backstory, rather than an invented family or military posting. His classic gossip also contrasts duty in Lordaeron with the threatened farms back home.

The present biography calls him Marshal and discusses the Westfall Brigade. Those later titles and deployments are not used for the NPC 234 entry. [The biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gryan_Stoutmantle) distinguishes original-game history from later events; its later material is a continuity check, not his current dialogue.

## Defias investigation

The Classic sequence follows Gryan's inquiry through Wiley the Black in Lakeshire, Wiley's note, Mathias Shaw in Stormwind, Shaw's report, the Defias Messenger, the escorted traitor and the Deadmines assignment. Some wiki chain summaries abbreviate the Shaw steps, so the individual quest records were also consulted.

- [First assignment (65)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Defias_Brotherhood_%28quest%29): Gryan seeks information from Wiley.
- [Shaw assignment (135), description and recipient dialogue](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/quest=135/the-defias-brotherhood): Gryan wants the Stonemasons lead checked. Shaw's progress and completion are not Gryan's lines.
- [Messenger assignment (142), description/progress/completion](https://www.60.tools/quests/142-the-defias-brotherhood): Gryan requests further evidence, then reports the captured thief's offer to reveal the hideout. The source identifies Forever's quest record with Classic details.
- [Chain synopsis](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Defias_Brotherhood_quest_chain) supplies context for the escort and dungeon transition; not every intermediate quest's full dialogue was retrievable.
- [Final assignment (166)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Defias_Brotherhood_%287%29): his description expresses regret at condemning a man, even while ordering VanCleef's death for Westfall's protection. The reward speech hopes for peace. This is evidence of a grave duty, not delight in killing.

The entry describes the investigation as his work without declaring VanCleef dead, the dungeon cleared or the reader inducted into the militia. It does not give Gryan foreknowledge of Vanessa or the political answers discovered later.

## Other relationships and Forever checks

[Messenger to Westfall](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Messenger_to_Westfall) places Magistrate Solomon's appeal before Gryan. The [recorded correspondence route](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/leveling/redridge/) returns Gryan's response, then continues to Darkshire. Available summaries explain that his own militia cannot safely leave Westfall; the entry avoids inventing a feud with Solomon or a promise of troops.

[Forever's NPC record](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/npcs/234) corroborates ID 234, Sentinel Hill and the militia/Defias quests, and lists the new Journey to Sentinel Hill turn-in. No unverified dialogue from that new introduction is invented. Level, coordinates, title and class remain live database observations rather than hard-coded additions to saved NPC data.

Later Northrend service, the promotion to Marshal, the Cataclysm attack and Legion events are excluded. Presence in a later time-travel dungeon does not establish his exact actions at Stratholme, so those are not invented. No separate book or comic scene was established as necessary for this entry.

The invitation to plant a row is a future hope, not an assertion that Westfall is already safe.
