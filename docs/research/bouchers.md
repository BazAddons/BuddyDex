# Keldric and Kyra Boucher

Researched November 8, 2026. **Keldric 1257**, human male, Arcane Goods Vendor; **Kyra 1275**, human female, Reagent Vendor. Both work in Pestle's Apothecary in Stormwind's Trade District with Morgan. Installed Scout independently records both IDs, names, current titles, sex, Humanoid type, Trade District and friendly reaction 5. Eligible through normal merchant interactions and emotes.

The wiki's broad Classic category mixes expansions; later powders, tomes and seasonal discoveries are not treated as original-era stock. [Keldric Classic reference on Forever maps](https://forevermastery.com/en/npcs/1257) explicitly labels its unconfirmed vendor data. Exact current quantities, prices and availability are not asserted in the profile.
- Shared surnames and a relative field do not establish which Boucher is spouse, parent or sibling. No exact relationship is invented. Neither is assigned a playable magical class merely for selling reagents.
- The similarly named [Boucher's Cauldron](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Boucher%27s_Cauldron) is a Booty Bay shop operated by other NPCs. Do not make it their former business or invent a history connecting them.
