## Master Gadrin

Confirmed: Darkspear elder, adviser to Vol'jin, opposes Zalazane and seeks release for his brother Minshina's trapped spirit. Recovering abandoned loa idols is an existing Forever commission. The profile does not assume the player's quests are already complete.

Read objective, description, progress and completion text where available:

Those other NPCs' instructions are not assigned to Gadrin.
- The complete venom/Shadra sequence: [2933](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2933), [2934](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2934), [2935](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2935), [2936](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2936), [2937](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2937), [2938](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2938). - [Forgotten Loa Idols 97225](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97225/forgotten-loa-idols): full published text, current API description and installed acceptance/completion dialogue. Eight abandoned idols are recovered; the profile invents no new loa, ritual powers or companion rewards.

The [original official manual](https://bnetcmsus-a.akamaihd.net/cms/template_resource/263A8NGR8HLZ1556919642368.pdf), Notable Trolls, supports Gadrin's advisory/teaching role. Relevant Gadrin passages in [Vol'jin: The Judgment](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/media/short-story/voljin-the-judgment) support his early teaching role and the tribe's departure. Only relevant passages, not the entire story, were reviewed. Future visions in that story are not current knowledge assigned to him. Later retail events, the later novel excerpt's post-Argus council, Zalazane's later return, Vol'jin's funeral and subsequent heritage events are excluded.

## Chief Hawkwind

Read full available quest text for [A Humble Task 752](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=752) and [753](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=753), [Rites of the Earthmother 755](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=755), [Rite of Strength 757](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=757) and [763](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=763). Also read Grull's related [747](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=747), [750](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=750), [780](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=780), and Hawkwind's [Attack on Camp Narache 781](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=781). Installed gossip reinforces eagerness tempered by wisdom. Greatmother's current node returning 404 does not establish death/removal; later Cataclysm family deaths and quests are excluded. No additional Classic personal novel/comic history was established.

Original writing: a small wooden leaf carved from scrap, including its uneven edge, and modest private reflections. It is not a new tribal totem or magic item. Favorite NOD is authored.

## Mai'ah

Confirmed: troll mage trainer near the Den in the Valley of Trials. Her teaching stresses patience and control rather than corruption. Classic troll pupils carry a tablet; Forever orc mage pupils carry a parchment. Befriending her does not grant class access or override trainer restrictions.

Read [Glyphic Tablet 3086](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3086), including objective, description, progress and completion, and the embedded page of [Glyphic Tablet 9575](https://classicdb.ch/?item=9575). Read all available stages of current [Glyphic Parchment 98576](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=98576/glyphic-parchment); installed acceptance/completion independently confirms the additional pupil route. Its sparse/404 API node is not used to deny the published quest. The new parchment's separate item page was not read, and its contents are not invented.

Installed gossip includes her mage greeting, refusal for other classes and the shared talent-retraining explanation. Shared trainer dialogue is not a unique biographical event. No separate Classic personal novel/comic history or family relationship was established.

Original writing: a mundane folded paper fan, finding the right angle for a breeze, and private friendship reflections. The fan has no spell, glyph or reward function. Favorite BOW is authored.
