## Coriella: both village tours

Her [objective relations](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/npc/254089/tab/objective-of) list [92514 Horde tour](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=92514/welcome-to-shendar-village) and [93461 Alliance tour](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=93461/welcome-to-shendar-village). Both are given and completed by Constable Aonda. The Horde version sends the player to Illaya Amberwind, the Alliance version to Rathiril Sunlance; both explicitly require Coriella as the innkeeper who can provide lodging and supplies. Aonda describes tension between the two groups while noting that they keep things civil in the village. These observations are not attributed as Coriella's personal political speech.

The preceding [Next Step 92472](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/quest/92472), reviewed with the Thendal guides, brings Aetheen's recommendation to Aonda. The linked follow-up [Criminal Element 92517](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/quest/92517) was reviewed through full current objective/description and installed completion: Aonda needs ten Highlands Bandits and their leader Badwind Bennic dealt with near the waterfall cave. Completion worries surviving bandits may join the cult. It is Aonda's combat assignment, not one given by Coriella, and is not used to claim the area is permanently safe.

Installed English NPC-keyed gossip welcomes a traveler to the Calmbreeze Inn and offers refreshment. Her [23-item current stock](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/npc/254089/tab/sells) includes food, drinks, Gustberry Pie/Juice, Pungent Skycheddar and stormapples. Stock is evidence of hospitality services, not proof she personally grows or cooks every item. No separate quest starts or ends appear in the consulted node; the objective relations are preserved rather than incorrectly labeling her questless.
