## Conservator Ilthalaine

Read full objective, description, progress and completion of [The Balance of Nature 456](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=456) and [457](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=457). Current [Forever 456](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=456/the-balance-of-nature) and [457](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=457/the-balance-of-nature) retain the same story; installed acceptance/completion independently matches. Historical comments have differing counts, so they are not used to rewrite objectives or turn the profile into a kill-count guide.

Confirmed: unusually heavy spring rains affected the balance between beasts; boar and nightsaber populations exceed available food. He treats thinning as a necessary burden for the shared forest, praises completed work and gives new adventurers an early task. This is not trophy hunting or a claim that BuddyDex friendship automatically resolves the forest's problem. His installed gossip explicitly appreciates Shadowglen's beauty, supporting a thoughtful naturalist voice.

## Dirania Silvershine

Read the complete four-stage chain, all available objective/description/progress/completion text: [A Good Friend 4495](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=4495), [A Friend in Need 3519](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3519), [Iverron's Antidote 3521](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3521), [3522](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3522). Checked corresponding current Forever pages [4495](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=4495/a-good-friend), [3519](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=3519/a-friend-in-need), [3521](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=3521/iverrons-antidote), [3522](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=3522/iverrons-antidote), and read the installed full acceptance/completion text for each.

Confirmed: Iverron is her friend and normally visits daily. She notices his absence and worries about the spider cave. Githyiss the Vile poisons him; she knows an antidote requiring hyacinth mushrooms, moonpetal lilies and webwood ichor. Her preparation must be delivered within five minutes. At the actual chain's conclusion he drinks it and feels better but remains seated. The authored biography describes her role without declaring that the present player has already saved him or that he is fully recovered. No romance, kinship, medical trainer profession, antidote handout or healing power is added.

No Dirania NPC-ID gossip entry was found in the installed corpus. Her modern returning-home/peaceful-woods gossip is associated with the later Cataclysm version and is not treated as verified Classic/Forever speech. Removed beta Vile Touch text, later bench placement and later uncertainty surrounding the Burning of Teldrassil are excluded.

Original writing: she deliberately gives a visitor the larger slice of bread while allowing them to think she simply cuts uneven portions. This small habit fits her care for a friend without inventing an inn, vendor, family relationship or cooking service. The rumor, private story, companion remarks and favorite HUG are authored. Friendship dialogue makes no promise of quest rewards or free items.
