## Malin: the complete Classic recovery path

Read the full objectives, descriptions, available progress and completion for:

- [Malin's Request 690](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=690): Malin's original master Trelane fled Stromgarde after failing to defend the Tower of Arathor. He wants belongings recovered; Malin has already sent agents and asks for extra help. It does not say Trelane died.
- [Worth Its Weight in Gold 691](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=691): Kryten's reagent commission, research notes, gratitude and description of Skuerto's scouting. Skuerto is explicitly a dwarf. This is the agents' field work, not proof Malin personally took the items.
- [Wand over Fist 693](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=693): Skuerto locates Trelane's wand with Kor'gresh and asks the player to focus on its retrieval. He advises food/rest before continuing.

The defenses are helpful rather than a guaranteed victory. A phylactery by itself does not establish that Trelane is a lich.

Malin's progress shows distraction and uncertain recognition; completion is delighted by the expedition's success and anticipation of the agents' return. The profile does not presume the player's character has brought the letter or completed the recovery.
