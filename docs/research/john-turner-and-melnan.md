# John Turner and Melnan Darkstone

Researched November 8, 2026. Installed Scout confirms **John Turner 6175**, male (sex 2), level 8, Alliance Humanoid, reaction 5, and **Melnan Darkstone 14842**, male (sex 2), level 30, Darkmoon Faire Barker, Humanoid, neutral reaction 4. Neither has an attackable flag; both were observed in Ironforge. They are individually named, so quests/conversation and friendly emotes qualify. Melnan is not the generic Darkmoon Faire Carnie. Classic models [John 4889](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=6175) and [Melnan 14878](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=14842) route to the established human male and dwarf male voices.

## John: charity and the dwarf paladin route

[Historical identity](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/John_Turner) identifies the human walking the Commons. The actual quest speech is the stronger source for his relationship and work. [Stephanie's biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stephanie_Turner) corroborates the wife named in John's dialogue. He is not assigned a priestly office, paladin class, age, or family relationship to other Turners based on a surname. No separate novel/comic narrative was established by targeted searches.

Full available objectives, descriptions, progress and completion were reviewed for the Classic route:

- [Introductory referral 2999](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2999), [Tiza's book grant 1645](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1645), and [book-started teaching 1646](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1646) establish Tiza Battleforge's responsibility and the importance of compassion alongside strength. [Tome of Divinity item 6916](https://classicdb.ch/?item=6916) was inspected for relevant book content. It starts a quest and the consulted page provides no separate readable Book pages. The quest description supplies the teaching; no claim is made to have read missing book pages.
- [1647](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1647) sends the paladin to find someone of good intent needing aid. John's completion interrupts his shouted orphanage appeal, mentions a long working day and missing his wife.
- [1648](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1648) seeks ten linen for clothes. John and his wife run a Stormwind orphanage, largely for children bereaved by Scourge attacks. Progress and completion express gratitude and his hope of returning home. [1778](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1778) names Stephanie and sends the paladin back to Tiza.
- [1779](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1779) gives the Symbol of Life and introduces Tiza's wounded husband Muiredon. [1783](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1783) sends aid to Narm Faulk after their encounter with Dark Iron dwarves. [1784](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1784) recovers a Dark Iron Script; [1785](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1785) returns to Tiza for Redemption. These tasks belong to the Battleforges, Narm and the player. John does not perform the resurrection, command the mission or claim its reward.

The preexisting installed **Spoken_Quests/Data/QuestText.lua** contains matching offer/completion text for John's 1648 and 1778 steps. [Forever player observation](https://ahledger.com/wow-forever/quests/1648-the-tome-of-divinity) reports his linen quest, and the [Forever identity/quest reference](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/6175/) lists his three direct steps while warning that positions mix sources. Scout verifies the current person, not every race restriction or reward. No player completion, donation button, new repeatable donation mechanism or completed shipment is assumed. Friendship through emotes is separate from the original paladin quest access.

The proposed Urheld Darkstone relationship is explicitly speculative and is omitted. Removal in retail patch 4.3 is not treated as his absence from Forever; Scout has the current sighting. Historical event timing sources disagree, so no exact spawn schedule is stated.

Full [quest 7905, The Darkmoon Faire](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=7905) objective, description, progress and completion were read: Melnan supplies a voucher to take to Gelvas Grimegate at the carnival; Gelvas handles its redemption. The Classic text points to the Faire outside Goldshire. Matching offer/completion text is present in installed **Spoken_Quests/Data/QuestText.lua**, entry 7905. This supports the traveling-Faire setting without promising that it is open in a particular place today. Prize-ticket redemption explains the referral; no larger chain is required or assigned to Melnan. Exact current rewards, timers, tickets or stock are omitted.

[Forever reference](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/map/ironforge/melnan-darkstone) corroborates his identity and quest association but does not replace current interaction evidence. He is not moved to Darkmoon Island or given later carnival attractions. Targeted book/comic searches did not establish a separate personal narrative. A private-server event-spawn issue is not evidence of Forever's schedule.
