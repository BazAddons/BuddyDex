# Innkeeper Farley: source and continuity notes

Researched November 8, 2026. NPC ID: **295**. Human innkeeper at the Lion's Pride Inn, Goldshire.

## Quest text and character evidence

### Rest and Relaxation (2158)

Falkhaan Isenstrider sends the adventurer south to Farley and identifies him as his best friend. The completion dialogue offers the weary arrival a place at the hearth and refreshments. This supports a hospitable host who understands that travelers need a break. The BuddyDex entry paraphrases the situation; its quote and companion dialogue are new writing.

- [Classic-era quest text, including completion, preserved by EvoWoW](https://wotlk.evowow.com/?quest=2158).
- [Warcraft Wiki quest page](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Rest_and_Relaxation). Its modern page also includes Cataclysm progression and later reward changes; those are not used for this entry.

### The Legend of Stalvan (69 and 70)

The Classic chain synopsis was read for context: Madame Eva's concern leads through Clerk Daltry's records and Moonbrook, then to Goldshire, Stormwind, Eastvale and back to Duskwood. Farley's evidence is one step in that investigation, not proof that he knows the final outcome.

The direct text of quest 70 recounts a messenger taking shelter in a storm, fleeing in fright at midnight and leaving correspondence upstairs. Farley retains it and lets the adventurer collect a letter for Stormwind's Canal District. Quest 70's progress/completion lines belong to its recipient, Caretaker Folsom, and must not be mistaken for Farley's voice. The chain later links papers through Folsom, Marshal Haggard, Tavernkeep Smitts and the Darkshire investigation.

- [Classic chain synopsis, Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Legend_of_Stalvan_quest_chain).
- [Quest 70 text, Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=70/the-legend-of-stalvan): description, objective, progress and completion available through search results. Direct Classic pages encountered a JavaScript verification screen.
- [Quest 70 text mirror, Kovarel](https://kovarel.com/wow-classic/quests/70-the-legend-of-stalvan).
- [Farley's step, Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Legend_of_Stalvan_%285%29).

This establishes Farley's memory for former guests and his custody of the correspondence. It does not establish that he caused the incident, read all the papers, met the chain's victims or knows how the mystery ends. None of those claims are added.

## Broader lore and timeline

[Warcraft Wiki's Farley biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fredrik_Farley) attributes his family history to *Before the Storm*, chapter 23: his given name is Fredrik, the inn is a longstanding family business, his wife is Verina, his daughter is Anna and his brother is Frandis. The novel itself was not directly inspected. Historical family ownership informs the backstory without assigning an unverified date to his taking over the inn. No new relatives or conflicting family history are invented.

The novel's post-Legion summons and Forsaken-related developments, as well as his later Arcantina appearance, are excluded from the Classic-era presentation. The public entry does not refer to his brother's later fate.

## Original BuddyDex additions

The lost-button gossip, guest-trinket habit, concern behind the secret, likes/dislikes, favorite emote and 15 companion lines are original writing. The entry moves from a small local joke about keeping lost belongings to the private reason he likes returning them: finding out whether the owner got home safely.

These habits extend the innkeeper and retained-letter facts without claiming new quest events. The favorite is THANK, a friendly emote supported by BuddyDex's gain handler; its hint invites the player to acknowledge his hospitality.

Farley's 15 personal lines are recorded with Eleven v3 in the existing custom BuddyDex Human Male (Steady) voice. Clips live under voice/human-male/lore295_*.ogg. No live in-game conversation or visual layout was inspected during this research pass.
