# Dungar Longdrink: source and continuity notes

Researched November 8, 2026. NPC **352**, human male, Stormwind gryphon master. No live in-game conversation or model was inspected in this pass.

## Classic courier chain: quest text

All four steps' objectives, descriptions, progress and completion text were read through the indexed Classic quest pages:

1. [A Swift Message (6181)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Swift_Message_%28Classic%29): Quartermaster Lewis requests armor through Osric Strang, a personal contact in Stormwind, and directs the courier to Thor. Thor offers transportation.
2. [Continue to Stormwind (6281)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Continue_to_Stormwind_%28Classic%29): Thor sends the courier to Osric's Old Town shop. Osric acknowledges Lewis's equipment needs and covers travel costs. Osric's comments about Stormwind's neglect are not Dungar's own dialogue.
3. [Dungar Longdrink (6261)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungar_Longdrink_%28Classic%29): Osric provides the return crate. Dungar's progress dialogue notices the courier's exertion and recommends a flight; his completion reassures the player that his gryphons know the Westfall route.
4. [Return to Lewis (6285)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Return_to_Lewis): Dungar names Thor and explains capital/remote route familiarity. Lewis receives the armor and encourages further help at Sentinel Hill. The thanks and armor distribution are Lewis's completion dialogue, not Dungar's.

These establish a brisk, confident instructor and the practical connection among Dungar, Thor, Osric and Lewis. The entry describes this ongoing role without claiming that this particular player has completed the delivery. It does not add flight mechanics, discounts or a new route requirement.

## Forever check and version traps

- [Forever quest record for 6261](https://senseos.gg/wiki/wow-forever/quests/6261/dungar-longdrink/) identifies the same four-step Westfall chain, citing build 1.60.1.70707. It supplies objectives and relationships, not giver dialogue.
- [Forever NPC record](https://www.stillsixty.com/npc/352) also links Dungar to Return to Lewis. The identity and service match the Classic source.
- [Modern quest rewrite](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungar_Longdrink_%28quest%29) substitutes Goldshire, Smith Argus and Bartlett. It is deliberately not used as the Classic courier story.
- [Character biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungar_Longdrink) also includes Lunarfall, Draenor quests and a modern route list. Those do not establish his earlier history. No current retail location, level or route list is copied into the entry.
- [Hearthstone disambiguation](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Dungar_Longdrink_%28disambiguation%29) identifies his card appearances. Hearthstone dialogue and joke characterizations are not treated as evidence for this WoW entry.

No separate Classic family history or origin was established in the consulted sources. The backstory therefore stays with ordinary working habits instead of inventing relatives, named past adventures or how he acquired his position.

## Original BuddyDex additions

His attention to buckles, parcels, clear explanations and inexperienced riders extends the job shown by the quest text. The nighttime singing rumor, supposed squeaky saddle, tuneless lullabies for hatchlings, CHEER favorite and 15 companion lines are original writing. The secret pays off the singing rumor; the Best Friend invitation lets the player join that quieter side of the job.

The friendly jab at Thor is consistent with Thor's existing BuddyDex references to Dungar; neither the rivalry nor the hatchling routine is claimed as official lore. New personal lines have speech bubbles but no custom recordings yet; general human male dialogue keeps existing voice support.
