## Dinita's full Classic quest context

Descriptions, progress where supplied, and completions were read directly over HTTP from ClassicDB:

1. [The Platinum Discs 2278](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2278): Uldaman's discs and stone watcher introduce the ancient lore repository and produce an accessible copy.
2. [The Platinum Discs 2279](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2279): the copy goes to High Explorer Magellas, whose enthusiasm and research claims are his, not Dinita's.
3. [The Platinum Discs 2439](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2439): Magellas sends a reward voucher to Dinita. Her progress is a professional welcome; completion recognizes the valuable authorization and offers the specified compensation. She is an administrator entrusted to pay the League's reward, not the archaeologist who discovered or interpreted the discs.
4. [Portents of Uldum 2963](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2963): Magellas refers the player to Karnik for further research.
5. [Seeing What Happens 2946](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2946), [The Stone Watcher 2954](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2954): the discs activate the Tanaris pedestal, but access requires the missing Plates of Uldum.
6. [Return to Ironforge 2977](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2977), [A Future Task 2964](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2964): the League has reached a temporary research impasse; this is not a Classic opening of the later retail Uldum zone. No such outcome is assigned to Dinita.

The Horde turn-ins are parallel alternatives, not Dinita's dialogue. Stone watcher instructions were reviewed as quest context; no claim is made that Dinita knows the entire repository's contents. The profile does not assume the reader completed any stage.

[Forever sequence record](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/series/2279/) supports the reward-voucher role while warning that sequence data alone does not prove prerequisites. [Forever location record](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/7292/) remains externally reported; the local Scout is the direct sighting evidence. Community pages that place Ironforge bank NPCs on a Dun Morogh map do not override the local city location. Exact rewards, prices and tuning are not reproduced in the profile.
