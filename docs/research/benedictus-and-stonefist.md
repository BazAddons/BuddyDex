Benedictus's ordinary glass bead, moving reflection and momentary distraction from a serious book are original neutral private pleasures. The bead is not the Eye of Paleth, an artifact, a promised gift, a hidden magical power or a claim about his lifelong morality. BOW acknowledges the archbishop's public role; it grants neither religious authority nor quest services.

Dashel's late-arriving retorts, quiet rehearsal and improved accounts of old arguments are original comic self-protection. They preserve his bluster without inventing more crimes, a redemption, a new accomplice or a completed player fight. FLEX suits his regard for strength. The companion's battle remark claims no real attack or damage contribution.
