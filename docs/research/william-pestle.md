# William Pestle: source and continuity notes

Researched November 8, 2026. NPC **253**, human male, apothecary staying at the Lion's Pride Inn, Goldshire. No live conversation or visual layout was inspected during this pass.

## Candle supply chain

- [Kobold Candles (60), text and completion](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Kobold_Candles): William explains that he and his brother run a Stormwind apothecary, requests candle wax and suggests the Elwynn mines. The modern page mixes later rewards into the record; those are not used. [Forever cache transcription, build 1.60.1.70862](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/quest/60/) corroborates the description and candle purpose.
- [Shipment to Stormwind (61), full quest text](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Shipment_to_Stormwind): William cannot make the trip himself and sends the candles to Morgan at Pestle's Apothecary in the Trade District. Progress and completion belong to Morgan, who receives the shipment and offers payment and goods. His sales pitch is not attributed to William.

These establish a practical business connection. No new method for using the wax in actual potion recipes is claimed.

## Lovers' chain and William's own words

The Classic sequence follows Maybell's letter to Tommy Joe, his appeal to his grandmother, her note to William, gathering kelp and delivering the invisibility liquor:

- [Young Lovers (106)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Young_Lovers): Maybell describes the family feud; Tommy Joe wants to see her.
- [Speak with Gramma (111)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Speak_with_Gramma): he asks the player to consult his grandmother; she looks for a temporary way to help.
- [Note to William (107)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Note_to_William): William recognizes Mildred as an old friend, sympathizes with the lovers and recalls having been young and in love himself. This is the principal evidence for his romantic, compassionate side.
- [Collecting Kelp (112), description, progress and completion](https://www.wowhead.com/quest=112/collecting-kelp): William explains the intended potion, asks for crystal kelp and gets to work once it arrives. [Forever description and completion](https://questcorehq.com/wowdb/forever/quest/112/collecting-kelp) supports the same role.
- [The Escape (114)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Escape_%28Alliance%29): William sends the liquor to Maybell. The conflicted thanks, decision to drink and departure are Maybell's response, not his. The entry does not assume this reader has completed the delivery.

The profile describes his role helping the lovers but does not imply that their feud is resolved, supply a marriage or family ending, or identify William's past sweetheart. Neither an old love nor Morgan's name is evidence that William was romantically involved with Mildred.

## Version and identity checks

- [William's biography and gossip](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/William_Pestle): identifies the profession, Morgan and a hospitable, enterprising manner. It also lists later family references; no new relatives are added to the entry.
- [Forever sighting record](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/npc/253/): records NPC 253 as a human man in the Lion's Pride Inn in build 1.60.1.70532. This is a third-party observation, not this agent's live verification.
- [A Visit With Maybell](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Visit_With_Maybell) was added in Cataclysm. Its explicit account of William overhearing Maybell is not assumed for Classic/Forever.
- One [Forever NPC listing](https://azeroth.wiki/forever/map/elwynn-forest/william-pestle) links **The Great Outdoors**, but accessible results did not establish William's personal dialogue in that quest. It is not used to invent another role or priority.
- The later Draenor continuation of the lovers' story is excluded. No relevant book or comic history was established in the consulted material; absence here is not proof that none exists.

## Original BuddyDex writing and recording selection

The notebook poetry, labeling habits, gossip, secret, LOVE favorite and 15 companion lines are original additions. The reserved recipe book turns out to contain sentimental verses, extending the empathy shown by Note to William without inventing a new romantic relationship. The Best Friend message invites trust rather than romance with the player.

Four signature lines are selected for Eleven v3 recording using the existing **BuddyDex Human Male (Steady)** voice: `greet:1`, `idle:1`, `befriended:1`, `quest:1`. Other personal lines remain speech bubbles; existing human male general lines still have recordings. Do not claim all 15 personal lines are voiced.
