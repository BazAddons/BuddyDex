# Lomac Gearstrip and Tinkmaster Overspark

Researched November 8, 2026. Installed Scout confirms **Lomac Gearstrip 4081**, male (sex 2), level 29, and **Tinkmaster Overspark 7944**, male (sex 2), level 57, Master Gnome Engineer. Both are named Alliance Humanoids, reaction 5, no attackable flag, Tinker Town, Ironforge. Classic displays [Lomac 2184](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=4081) and [Overspark 7028](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=7944) route to the established male gnome voice. Quests, conversation/training and friendly emotes qualify. The generic names Dwarf Guard, Ironforge Guard and Stormwind City Guard were separately rechecked against the runtime exclusion and remain rejected.

## Lomac's Classic chain

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Lomac_Gearstrip) identifies the male gnome, his old friend Gaxim Rustfizzle and NG-5. Full Classic objectives, descriptions, progress and completion were read for:

- [On Guard in Stonetalon 1070](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1070), [1085](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1085), and [A Gnome's Respite 1071](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1071): Thenysil, Kaela and Gaxim establish the Venture Co. threat and Gaxim's desire to combine magic, alchemy and engineering. Those are Gaxim's claims and relationships, not evidence Lomac personally fought in the forest.
- [An Old Colleague 1072](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1072) names Lomac as Gaxim's old friend and source of potent NG-5. Lomac's completion is suspicious and abrasive. [Ineptitude + Chemicals = Fun 1073](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1073) requests the potions, shows his grim amusement at the experiment and gives credit for alchemical competence. [1074](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1074) sends the volatile material back to Gaxim and includes a greeting for his old colleague. This is the basis for his sharp humor; the profile does not turn him into an earnest safety lecturer.
- Parallel preparation: [A Scroll from Mauren 1075](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1075), [Devils in Westfall 1076](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1076), and [Special Delivery for Gaxim 1077](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1077) establish Collin Mauren's messaging spell for Gaxim's remote device. [Retrieval for Mauren 1078](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1078) was checked as related context. The spell, device placement and reagent commission are not assigned to Lomac.
- Follow-through: [Covert Ops Alpha 1079](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1079), [Beta 1080](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1080), [Kaela's Update 1091](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1091), [Enraged Spirits 1083](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1083), [Wounded Ancients 1084](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1084), [Update for Sentinel Thenysil 1082](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1082), and [Reception from Tyrande 1081](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=1081) were read. The operation seeks plans and letters, then investigates damage in the Charred Vale and reports to the Sentinels. It does not prove Lomac himself healed the land or performed the infiltration. The fictional explosive is not converted into real chemistry instructions.

The installed **Spoken_Quests/Data/QuestText.lua** has matching offer/completion passages for 1072-1074. The installed audio quest-NPC lookups assign the two direct Lomac quests to his identity. [Forever NPC reference](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/4081/) corroborates the direct three-step chain while warning that reported positions mix sources. [External reference](https://forevermastery.com/en/npcs/4081) explicitly identifies its Classic base. No current reward, access restriction or exact coordinate is promised, and no player completion is assumed.

The relevant readable [Purchase Order Receipt](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Purchase_Order_Receipt) was inspected in full. It is authored by Lomac but added in **patch 4.0.3a**, involving the Gadgetsprings and a giant. That episode is excluded. No separate personal novel/comic narrative was established by targeted searches.

## Overspark's Classic work

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tinkmaster_Overspark) distinguishes original-world engineering instruction and Techbot from his later expansion appearances. [Brother Sarno's referral 2923](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2923) describes the inventor's distress; [Save Techbot's Brain 2922](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2922) requests the memory core of the malfunctioning creation. All objective, description, progress and completion passages were read. Completion begins work and offers hopeful reassurance; **it does not demonstrate a completed repair**. The profile retains that uncertainty. Matching text is in the installed quest corpus, including the specific core-return passage, and the audio lookup maps it to 7944.

His engineering specialization material was already read in [Jemma/Springspindle research](jemma-and-springspindle.md): Springspindle's referral 3632, the pledge 3640, sample work 3641, renewal 3645 and all seven pages of the in-game Manual of Engineering Disciplines 10789. This turn also read the full [Lilliam referral 3630](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3630) and [Tinkerwiz referral 3634](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3634). Their different partisan introductions lead to the same trainer. Overspark's dramatic response to Gnomeregan's loss, enthusiasm for students and insistence on discretion inform the writing. His professional use of brothers/sisters is not a new family relationship. Old lifetime exclusivity and renewal rules are historical text, not current Forever advice.

[Forever quest reference](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/7944/) corroborates the core-return and specialization paths without independently confirming every current mechanic. Later Pandaria, Stormheim, Mechagon and Khaz Algar events, Gila's later expedition role, and Hearthstone's squirrel-stampede flavor are excluded. A TCG card/search hit is not treated as a new Classic personal event. Oglethorpe's grievance is not resolved or turned into an invented reconciliation. No separate relevant Classic novel/comic episode was established.

Lomac's retained thank-you note, affection disguised as impatience, preferences and private paper-keeping habit are original additions around his old-colleague relationship.
