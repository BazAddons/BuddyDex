# Melika and the upstairs trainers

Researched November 8, 2026. Installed Scout confirms four named, non-attackable Humanoids, reaction 5, at the Lion's Pride Inn: **Melika Isenstrider 6778**, female, Assistant Innkeeper; **Michelle Belle 2329**, female, Physician; **Priestess Josetta 377**, female, Priest Trainer; **Zaldimar Wefhellt 328**, male, Mage Trainer. All are human. Normal friendly conversation/service/quest/emote eligibility applies; a named NPC does not need a dialogue window to qualify through emotes.

ClassicDB model IDs: [Melika 5527](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=6778), [Michelle 1296](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=2329), [Josetta 1295](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=377), [Zaldimar 1294](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=328). Bundled race/sex metadata and runtime routing are checked before deployment.

## Melika

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Melika_Isenstrider) establishes the assistant innkeeper in the main room. The relative field names Falkhaan without specifying the relationship; none is invented. Her original-beta role ending Rest and Relaxation is not assumed active now. [Classic quest 2158](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2158) was read: Falkhaan advertises the inn, names Farley as his best friend, and Farley's completion welcomes the weary traveler. These statements do not make Melika Farley's spouse, Falkhaan's wife/sister, or the current quest recipient. No direct quest starts/ends were listed for her on the consulted ClassicDB NPC page. No verified new personal Forever chain was found.

## Michelle and Forever First Aid

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Michelle_Belle) establishes her upstairs location beside Josetta and historical first-aid gossip. Its current retail title Bandage Trainer and tailor-only description are not substituted for the installed Scout's **Physician** title or Forever's First Aid profession. Real-world naming trivia is not a character biography.

Her role is training/turn-in, not Sam's introductory speech or invention of the campsite system. The preceding Adventurer and Great Outdoors quest texts and on-accept instruction were reviewed in the [previous staff research](lions-pride-staff.md); the Great Outdoors text was also reread for this batch. Parallel profession/racial lessons are alternatives, not Michelle's personal history. No locally captured quest dialogue is claimed, and exact skill thresholds/tuning are omitted from the profile.

## Josetta's Classic lessons and Forever referrals

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Priestess_Josetta) establishes her priest teaching, shared upstairs room and the source for her unfulfilled travel wish. Full available descriptions, progress and completion were read from:

- [In Favor of the Light 5623](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5623): Anetta sends a pupil to Josetta; Josetta welcomes further teaching.
- [Garments of the Light 5624](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5624): heal and strengthen **Guard Roberts**, then return. Josetta emphasizes useful healing and Fortitude, the town's safety, and a first robe that signifies church approval. She does not require the robe for later tests.
- [Returning Home 5628](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5628), cross-checked against the [Elwynn-specific transcription](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Returning_Home_(Elwynn_Forest)): Josetta admires a traveling night elf and wishes she had traveled when younger, then relays Alathea's summons. Alathea's welcome and [Stars of Elune](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Stars_of_Elune) completion are Alathea's lesson, not evidence Josetta practices Elune's racial spell.
- [Desperate Prayer, Goldshire](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desperate_Prayer_(Goldshire)) and [Cathedral Square lesson](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Desperate_Prayer_(Cathedral_Square)): Classic referral to Laurena and her instruction. Several ClassicDB NPC start-list links led to other trainers' variants; variant accents and wording are not attributed to Josetta from that list. Her actual Goldshire transcription is used instead. Old spell availability is not promised in Forever.
- Forever [Divine Grace 94774](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94774): Josetta's description sends a promising human priest to Laurena, whose completion begins advanced instruction. [Confounding Flash 94822](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94822) similarly directs a gnome priest to Mims in Ironforge; Mims's completion asks for attention to the lesson. [Mims's direct lesson 94817](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=94817) corroborates the recipient. No separate progress passage was exposed for these short referrals. These are alternatives for different pupils, not a chain in which everyone learns every racial spell.

The profile uses the stable mentoring role and the explicit Classic travel wish. It does not promise exact racial rewards, declare every old referral currently available, or assume the player has completed any lesson.

The biography cites **Stormrage**: its apparent necromancer betrayal is explicitly in **Dughan's nightmare** during later events. The indexed novel excerpt also places the apparent betrayal in that scene; it is not proof that the real Zaldimar secretly practices necromancy. No later Nightmare event or treachery is added to the Forever profile. Zalna's speculative kinship is omitted. Targeted searches did not establish additional personal novel/comic history for the other three; location appearances alone do not create character actions.
