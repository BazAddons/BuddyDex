## The lovers' Classic chain

Objectives, descriptions, progress where supplied, and completions were read directly over HTTP for the complete five-step chain:

1. [Young Lovers 106](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=106): Maybell asks for her letter to be delivered because the family feud keeps her from Tommy Joe. His response expresses his own longing, not hers.
2. [Speak with Gramma 111](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=111): Tommy Joe trusts his grandmother to help; her completion hopes for a temporary meeting despite the feud. The ClassicDB progress snippet appears unrelated to the surrounding text, so it is not used for characterization.
3. [Note to William 107](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=107): Gramma sends the player to William Pestle. William recognizes Mildred as an old friend and sympathizes with the lovers.
4. [Collecting Kelp 112](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=112): William explains the invisibility liquor, requests kelp and prepares it. Those are his alchemical skills, not Gramma's or Maybell's.
5. [The Escape 114](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=114): Maybell feels guilty about deceiving family but intends to visit her love. The entry does not assume the player has delivered it or resolve the feud.

[Gramma Stonefield's Note](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gramma_Stonefield%27s_Note) was also read: it names Mildred, identifies Tommy Joe as her grandson, mentions her old friendship with William and his brother, and asks that young love be nurtured amid threatening news. It invites an old friend's visit, not proof of a past romance between them.

[Gramma biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Gramma_Stonefield) supports her opposition to the feud. [Tommy Joe biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Tommy_Joe_Stonefield) separates Classic from the later Warlords marriage and barn work. That marriage, Maybell's later surname and Draenor animal-capture lines are excluded. The Cataclysm breadcrumb *A Visit With Maybell* is not inserted before the Classic chain.

[Forever quest observations](https://www.thelongroad.io/quest) list the same local quests; [Forever Classic-route transcription](https://foreverupgrades.com/quest.html?id=106) explicitly warns its source has not been independently checked in Forever. The latter also maps the route into Duskwood, conflicting with local Scout/established Elwynn placement, so those coordinates are not used. No new Forever family outcome was established.

## Bernice's necklace and pie chain

All four steps were reviewed in full:

1. [Lost Necklace 85](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=85): Bernice suspects Billy and identifies herself as a widow. His denial is self-serving; do not treat either accusation or denial as an omniscient account.
2. [Pie for Billy 86](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=86): Billy requests her pork belly pie for information. Bernice agrees reluctantly and explains her cooking method in completion.
3. [Back to Billy 84](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=84): her sharp send-off and his enthusiastic response establish both her temper and her baking reputation. BuddyDex does not repeat the original hostile wish verbatim.
4. [Goldtooth 87](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=87): Billy reports seeing the necklace taken into the mine. Bernice's grateful completion gives an item that belonged to her late husband, who had left it behind on his last campaign. The husband's keepsake reward is not identified as her missing necklace.

[Bernice biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/%22Auntie%22_Bernice_Stonefield) corroborates the widow, farm and pies. No husband name, campaign identity, military unit or exact family relationship to the other Stonefields is invented. Her irritation with Billy remains; she is not rewritten as already having forgiven the feud. Her Scout location label does not establish that she explores the mine.
