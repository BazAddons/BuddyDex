## Full main quest text

Read objectives, descriptions, progress where present and completion on the primary Classic pages, with current Forever descriptions/objectives and full installed accept/completion blocks corroborating the first five:

- [Rude Awakening 363](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=363): Mordo identifies himself as caretaker of Deathknell's crypt, notices the new arrival can respond, and sends them to Sarvis. His threatened fire disposal is blunt, macabre context. Sarvis explains Sylvanas freed the Forsaken from the Lich King's control and their enemies include Scourge and hostile humanity. This is not the later Val'kyr raising scene.
- [The Mindless Ones 364](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=364): Sarvis demands eight Mindless Zombies and eight Wretched Zombies; he regards the mindless Scourge as enslaved former kin who cannot join the Forsaken. Progress is stern about competence. Completion regrets their numbers cannot be recruited, without offering a cure.
- [Rattling the Rattlecages 3901](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=3901): twelve skeletons test the recruit under greater pressure. Sarvis insists on effort; completion recognizes demonstrated worth and insists the Forsaken will remain free. His companion warmth does not erase his demanding public role.
- [The Damned 376](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=376): Elreth explicitly tends wounded warriors, tailors clothes and armor and assists Sarvis. Six paws and six wings procure armor. Her progress asks the recruit to remain unharmed; completion includes a dry remark about the previous wearer. Do not turn her into a formal tailoring trainer or claim the gathered materials have a particular medical recipe.
- [Marla's Last Wish 6395](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=6395): after The Damned, Elreth asks for Samuel Fipps' remains to be buried in Marla's grave. Marla is explicitly Elreth's friend. Samuel became Scourge and killed Marla; Marla died without becoming undead and wished for burial with him. Elreth's progress distinguishes respectful remembrance from the Scourge, and completion hopes for peace. The profile does not presume this player has done the quest, raise the couple, describe a new reunion or invent an Elreth/Fipps family link.
