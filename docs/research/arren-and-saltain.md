Researched November 9, 2026. Both profiles are written with fifteen lines and four directed signature scripts; recordings remain pending the official Forsaken voice choice. They must not be counted complete yet.

## Identity and eligibility

Scout observes Executor Arren **1570**, male level 5, eleven sightings, and Deathguard Saltain **1740**, male level 7, thirteen sightings, in Deathknell, Tirisfal Glades/map 1420 on November 8. Both are normal Horde-friendly (reaction 5) Humanoids (type 7), without an attackable flag. Their titles accompany individual names, so generic guard exclusions do not apply.

Current Forever nodes independently confirm undead race, levels and friendliness: [Arren](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/npc/1570), display **1583**, and [Saltain](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/npc/1740), display **3523**, both character models. No exact-ID installed gossip or chatter block was found for either. Their established public voices therefore come from their quest text, not invented ambient quotations.

## Forever referral and its camping follow-through

The current Arren node exposes five starts, the four Classic stages plus **The Adventurer 96656**. Read [the primary Forever quest](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96656/the-adventurer), its current database node and the full installed accept/completion block. Arren trusts a recruit after their Scarlet efforts and asks them to check on Eleanor Shackleton near Brill. Eleanor denies Scarlet affiliation and offers camping instruction. This is a specific referral, not proof Arren personally teaches camping, knows every camping profession or gives camping powers as a buddy. No separate progress passage is exposed.

Checked Eleanor **265812** current starts/ends and followed her accessible branch:

- [The Great Outdoors 96607](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96607/the-great-outdoors): sit at her Basic Campfire and receive Boosted Rest; description and progress stress outdoor preparedness. Completion directs the learner to profession trainers for camp fixtures.
- [Camping 101: Cooking 96658](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=96658/camping-101-cooking): learn cooking from William Pickman in Brill, with a campfire as the foundation. Progress and completion explain basic furnishing limits and later improvements through cooking.
- [Camping 101: First Aid 97955](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=97955/camping-101-first-aid): reach skill 20 and consult Nurse Neela in Brill for a First Aid Kit. Full text explains the practical healing role, wilderness testing and prerequisite campfire.
- [Camping 101: Mining 97959](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/quest/97959): reach skill 20 and consult Brom Killian in Undercity for a Lodestone. The primary Wowhead page returned 403; the current node provides objectives/description and the full installed accept/completion block explains the fixture and wilderness/campfire requirements. No separate progress passage was available in these fallback sources, so one is not invented.
- [Sticks and Bones 86784](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=86784/sticks-and-bones): six Dry Branches go to Eleanor, who welcomes extra wood and lets the traveler retain the collecting basket. Full objective, description, progress and completion were read, plus the installed block. This related return is not attributed to Arren; its description calls Eleanor the speaker's associate without independently establishing Arren as that speaker.

All five camping quest installed blocks were read in full. Comments about bugs, profession thresholds or farming locations are not incorporated as permanent mechanics or personal history. No readable camping letter/book was exposed in these steps. Coverage of this referral does not claim every camping quest across all profession trainers has been researched; the profile uses only Arren's verified referral.

## Canon boundaries and original details

The [Arren biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Executor_Arren) explicitly labels the *Lands of Conflict* patrol betrayal/ogre necromancer story and prejudice against humans/dwarves as non-canon RPG material. Those events and a supposed keep are excluded. His later retail field position, altered quest sequence and beta-only Beat Them, Then Eat Them are also excluded. The [Saltain biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathguard_Saltain) lists later Recruitment/Beyond the Grave/Shadow Priest Sarvis quests; they are not substituted for his verified Forever salvage quest. No relevant canonical personal comic/novel episode was established for either.

Arren's overly elaborate private walking plan, pared down to time with a friend, is original. It is not a new campaign or his neglect of public duties. SALUTE reflects clear purpose and follow-through. Saltain's kept spare button, valued simply for its color, is original and adds no item gift, required inventory, family heirloom or new profession. THANK acknowledges useful assistance. Their friendlier personal dialogue retains the plain, gruff voices of their quests. Written profiles remain marked unfinished until approved race voices are recorded and verified.
