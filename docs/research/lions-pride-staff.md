[Skin of Sweet Rum](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=1939/skin-of-sweet-rum) identifies him as the vendor for one item needed in **Dry Times**. All descriptions, progress and completion text were read directly from [Dry Times 116](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=116) and its linked supplier task [Thunderbrew 117](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=117). Daniels needs several drinks because a shipment is late; the player sources them across the region. Grimbooze's hops-and-lager exchange belongs to Grimbooze, not Dobbins. Daniels's gratitude and seamstress wife belong to Daniels. Dobbins supplies one item; he is not given the whole distribution operation or the other man's family.

[Captain Washburn's account](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Captain_Washburn) mentions Dobbins but belongs to Warlords content (6.0.2), including the arrow-to-the-knee story. It is not inserted into this Classic/Forever profile. No direct Classic quest starts/ends were listed on Dobbins's consulted NPC page. Exact stock, prices and item tuning are not promised for Forever.

## Tomas and Forever camping

Tomas's biography supplies his cooking-trainer role and welcoming learner gossip. The name's possible real-world Thomas Cook reference is not an in-world travel career.

The new Forever lesson is included, based on the following external quest-text records:

1. [The Adventurer 96627](https://www.stillsixty.com/quest/96627): McBride sends the player to investigate Sam Sarsaparilla; Sam responds by offering camping instruction. Objective, description and completion were read. No separate progress passage was supplied by that page. [ForeverDB's observations](https://foreverdb.net/fr/quest/96627-the-adventurer) corroborate McBride-to-Sam and report Skirmish at Echo Ridge as preceding it; these links are not assigned to Tomas as personal deeds.
2. [The Great Outdoors](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Great_Outdoors_(Forever)): objective, full Elwynn description, progress, completion and on-accept instruction were read. Sam teaches resting near a fire and how trades contribute fixtures, then directs players toward village trainers. His instructional speech remains his, not Tomas's. Other racial variants are alternatives.
3. [Camping 101: Cooking](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Camping_101:_Cooking): objective, description, progress and completion were read. Cooking supplies the initial fire, with more possibilities as skill improves. [Elwynn quest 96626](https://forever-xp.com/quests/96626/) corroborates Sam-to-Tomas. The description is Sam's offer; Tomas is the lesson recipient and cooking trainer. The profile does not claim Sam taught him or that Tomas invented camping. Broader profession branches are parallel lessons, not extra personal history.

The chain is externally documented for Forever, not locally captured quest dialogue. Timing, furnishings, costs, experience and exact prerequisite tuning are omitted. Earlier Northshire combat tasks do not establish Tomas's participation. No assumption is made that a player has completed the camping lesson.
