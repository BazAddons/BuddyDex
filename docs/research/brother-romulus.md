# Brother Romulus

Researched November 9, 2026. This is an example where a Season of Discovery origin is not grounds to discard an NPC actually observed in Forever. The profile uses his local identity and bounded dialogue, while keeping the event's original rune reward and later developments separate.

## Verified identity and eligibility

Installed Scout records **Brother Romulus 205278**, level 30, male (unit sex 2), normal Alliance-friendly (reaction 5) Humanoid (type 7), three sightings in the Cathedral of Light, Stormwind City/map 1453, without an attackable flag. He has an individual name and is eligible through friendly interactions or emotes even without a normal quest-start table.

The [current Forever node](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/api/data/node/npc/205278) identifies a human with display **11350**, and the [Forever Wowhead page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=205278/brother-romulus) places him in Stormwind. Client body metadata independently routes his display to human male. His current node omits level/reaction and exposes screenshots/comments only; Scout provides the observed values. The [biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Brother_Romulus) records his original addition in SoD 1.15.0 and his Cathedral priest role. The actual Forever sighting is stronger evidence of current presence than an old wiki's mode label.

## Entire available personal conversation

Read both exact-ID lines in installed `SpokenQuestsAudioGossip/generated/npc_gossip_file_lookups.lua`: his short worry about Sister Ada and the longer report of her disappearance. The second calls her a young class-token character; the [original transcription](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Brother_Romulus) identifies that class as paladin. He says she vanished the previous night, was last seen in the Cathedral catacombs, and other brothers noticed withdrawn, shifty behavior. He ends with a wish for her protection and acknowledges the limit of what they know.

The profile does not turn the relative phrase last night into a permanent calendar statement every time the Dex opens. Calling her Sister does not establish a biological sibling. Other brothers' impressions remain reports, not a diagnosis or proof Romulus personally knows her intentions. No exact-ID installed chatter or conventional quest block naming him or Ada was found in the checked `Spoken_Quests` corpus. This is a gossip-driven mystery rather than a verified sequence of normal Romulus quest IDs.

## Original character writing and verification

His preference for distinguishing rumor from certainty, thoughts about how to welcome a worried returnee, and imagined first question about whether Ada has eaten are modest original internal characterization around his demonstrated concern. They are conditional hopes, not a visit that occurred or a guaranteed future rescue. There is no new meal, chair object, missing-person tracker, quest flag, dialogue branch, cult reward or summon of Ada. The buddy does not know she is safe, dead, rescued or at home.

COMFORT recognizes his worry. The `comforted` line is written for Romulus comforting the crying player, the direction in which that companion moment actually works; it does not mistakenly have him thank the player for comforting him. Friendship welcomes company without assuming the player is a paladin, investigated the crypt or completed an encounter.
