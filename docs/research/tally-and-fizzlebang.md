Normal training/vendor/conversation and friendly-emote eligibility applies. No friendship is awarded merely for a Scout sighting.

## Tally: teacher and colleague

[Classic NPC](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=5177) and its embedded lists show alchemy training without personal quest starts or ends. [Biography and gossip](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Tally_Berryfizz) establish her work in Berryfizz's Potions and Mixed Drinks and explicitly describe Vosur Brakthel as both apprentice and friend. [Vosur's reciprocal gossip](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Vosur_Brakthel) credits her expertise and says he learns from her daily. This is the relationship used in the profile. No invented apprenticeship origin, romance or familial connection is added. The pages' direct opening was blocked but full indexed text was available.

Tally's biography specifically places the proposed Soolie relationship under **relative speculation**. Soolie's page lists Tally as a relative without that qualification. The conflict is resolved conservatively: the profiles use their shared workplace, not kinship. The earlier Soolie notes have been clarified accordingly. Name similarity alone is insufficient.

The [published original-world atlas index](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/connected/wowatlas/RegionalIndices_hr.pdf) places Tally's alchemy training and Soolie's supplies in the same shop. This is an occupation/location reference, not a narrative. Targeted searches did not establish a personal book/comic story.

[Forever's NPC/trainer listing](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=5177/tally-berryfizz) shows changed names and new offerings compared with Classic; some other Forever sites explicitly use unverified historical data. Local Scout confirms her current identity and title. The profile makes no exact claim about skill caps, present fees, recipe counts or the old allocation of healing recipes. It does not transfer retail mastery quests to Forever. No direct personal Forever chain was established by targeted searches or the installed SpokenQuestsAudioAlliance/Shared/Gossip quest-NPC mappings. Thus there is no verified direct chain whose quest dialogue can be analyzed here; her own and Vosur's gossip supply the characterization.

## Fizzlebang: year-round fireworks

[Classic NPC](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=5569) supplies the model and vendor identity; embedded related lists have no direct quest starts/ends. [Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Fizzlebang_Booms) places him at **Things That Go Boom!** year-round. This distinguishes him from holiday-only sellers. Its possible relationship to Wizbang Booms is speculation and is omitted. No connection to Wilfred or Lulubelle Fizzlebang is inferred from a shared name. The Legion quest Finding Fizzlebang is unrelated later content, not this merchant's quest.

[Forever reference](https://forevermastery.com/en/npcs/5569) lists a red fireworks rocket while labeling the base NPC/vendor data historical and requiring confirmation. [Another Forever reference](https://metaforge.app/wow-forever/database/npcs/fizzlebang-booms) also lists the fireworks trade and acknowledges VMaNGOS data. Exact stock, price, fireworks mechanics, seasonal events and a particular route are not asserted. A generic quest-giver badge without a quest list is not evidence of a personal chain. No direct chain was found in the Classic lists, targeted Forever searches or installed quest-NPC mappings.

Targeted book/comic searches did not establish another personal history. A World Anvil tabletop adaptation invents a robotic arm, eye patch and different business details; it is fan material and is excluded. No injury or prosthetic is assigned to him.
