# Auctioneers Buckler and Redmuse

Researched November 8, 2026. Installed Scout records **Auctioneer Buckler 8671**, male (sex 2), and **Auctioneer Redmuse 8720**, female (sex 3), both level 50, Alliance Humanoids, reaction 5, no attackable flag, Ironforge. They are individually named and eligible through the auction service, conversation or friendly emotes. [Classic Buckler](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=8671) uses display **7990**; [Classic Redmuse](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=8720) uses **7989**. Runtime metadata routes these to the established male and female dwarf voices. Neither is the female gnome **Lympkin 9859**.

## Established roles and location

[Buckler biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auctioneer_Buckler) identifies the male dwarf auctioneer in the Commons. [Redmuse biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auctioneer_Redmuse) and the [older identity reference](https://wowwiki-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Auctioneer_Redmuse) identify the female dwarf in the Commons. [The auctioneer list](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auctioneer) places them alongside Lympkin. Their shared workplace does not establish family ties, romance, a feud, prior military service or a particular birthplace. Comments about Buckler's popularity with women are not canonical relationship evidence.

The Classic NPCs' embedded related lists have no personal quest starts or ends. Targeted Forever searches and installed quest-NPC mappings did not establish a new direct quest chain, special dialogue or a personal novel/comic story. That is a bounded search result, not proof that no additional material exists. There is no verified direct chain whose objective, description, progress and completion can add personal characterization here. The entries therefore use their established service and location, with modest original writing.

[Forever Buckler reference](https://forevermastery.com/en/npcs/8671) and [Redmuse reference](https://forever-tavern.com/npcs/classic/8720-auctioneer-redmuse/) acknowledge their historical data and the need for confirmation. The installed Scout supplies current local identity, reaction, sex and level. External world-coordinate pages labeling the surrounding area Dun Morogh do not override the local Ironforge sighting. Exact coordinates, auction prices, durations, house fees, faction access changes and modern auction mounts are not taught by the profiles.

## Original BuddyDex writing

Buckler's preference for orderly bids, sympathy toward the breakfast seller, quiet conversation and all private anecdotes are original. His name does not make him an armor merchant or establish a military past. Redmuse's interest in overlooked objects, private notebook of imaginary household-object adventures and thoughtful conversation are original. Her fictional notebook is explicitly not provenance used to market auction listings. No item is assigned a canonical history through those imagined stories. All quotes and fifteen personal lines per NPC are newly written.

## Correcting the test identities

The main smoke fixture previously called NPC **8720** Lympkin. This would make Redmuse's authored profile silently affect tests intended for a different NPC. The fixture now uses **9859**, Lympkin's actual ID, including its GUID and references. The favorite-emote test now supplies the NPC ID and exercises Lympkin's authored **APPLAUD** preference rather than the generic auctioneer fallback. The no-lore UI case uses a synthetic unauthored identity so future real profiles do not invalidate its premise. Scout's legacy import fixture keeps real **8720** but correctly names Redmuse and uses display **7989**. No saved variables were rewritten to alter real NPC identities.

Both new profiles receive four directed Eleven v4 recordings using the selected dwarf male/female voices. Automated layout, supported moments, accepted favorites, metadata, mapping, decoding, runtime first-welcome playback and smoke checks are separate from human listening and live visual review.
