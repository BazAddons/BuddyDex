## Duesten's complete Beryl branch

Read [In Favor of Darkness 5651](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5651) in full and its installed accept/completion block. Duesten explicitly teaches that light and dark both deserve understanding and discretion, then refers the pupil to Dark Cleric Beryl in Brill. Beryl requires urgency and ambition as well as faith.

Followed the entire next quest, [Garments of Darkness 5650](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=5650), reading objective, description, progress and completion. Beryl asks the priest to heal Deathguard Kel using Lesser Heal rank 2 and fortify him with Power Word: Fortitude. Her progress values healing and endurance without prohibiting other paths; completion grants a robe as recognition without requiring it be worn. These are Beryl's instructions and the player's work, not a rescue performed by Duesten himself. This prevents flattening his theology into a blanket refusal to heal or a claim that only dark damage is valid.
