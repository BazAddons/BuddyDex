## Shoni: complete quest text and the limits of progress

Read full objectives, descriptions, available progress and completion for [Speak with Shoni 2041](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2041), [Underground Assault 2040](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2040) and [Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators 2928](https://classicdb.ch/?quest=2928). The first two are Gnoarn's referral and its follow-up, previously documented in [Gnoarn research](gnoarn-and-gearcutter.md), rechecked here. He identifies her as commander of the underground assault crew. She seeks an intact shredder power supply from the Deadmines for the digging machines. The later robot-parts request concerns getting the fleet to Dun Morogh; her completion reports a single repaired machine and hundreds remaining. That sharp progress remark informs her impatience, not a claim that the fleet or city is ready. The writing does not presume the player made either delivery or liberated Gnomeregan.

All three offers/completions are present in the installed Spoken quest corpus. Classic related lists establish direct identity associations. Current Forever node counts alone are not proof of identical present-day chain availability, rewards or restrictions. Those are not promised. Jemma's new archive quest was checked in [her separate research](jemma-and-springspindle.md); an external chain display does not make Shoni the owner of those archives. Her own complete installed gossip entry says only that the cause is Gnomeregan. The nickname does not establish an inability to speak; her quest text plainly contains speech.

The relevant *Even More Magic & Mayhem* passage about [Shoni's Disarming Tool](https://anyflip.com/jdspb/fged/basic/151-197) describes an RPG version of her as a rogue and attributes a specialized tool to her. That passage was inspected, but its RPG biography/mechanics are not imported. Blizzard's [Ask Creative Development, Round II answer](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2721372142-ask-creative-development-round-ii-answers/) says RPG material should be treated as non-canonical unless separately established. The actual game quest reward bearing her name does not independently verify every RPG biographical detail. No separate canonical novel/comic narrative was established.
