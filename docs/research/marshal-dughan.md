# Marshal Dughan: source and continuity notes

Researched November 8, 2026. NPC **240**, human male, Stormwind marshal in Goldshire. No live in-game conversation was inspected in this pass.

## Classic quest evidence

- **The Fargodeep Mine (62) -> The Jasperlode Mine (76):** Dughan requests reconnaissance and reacts unhappily to confirmed kobold occupation. Progress text asks what the scout found. Completion dialogue mixes exasperation with practical concern for patrols. [Fargodeep text](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Fargodeep_Mine), [Jasperlode text](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Jasperlode_Mine).
- **A Fishy Peril -> Further Concerns -> Find the Lost Guards -> Discover Rolf's Fate -> Report to Thomas -> Deliver Thomas' Report -> Cloth and Leather Armor:** Remy brings a warning, Dughan wants military confirmation, and Guard Thomas's investigation establishes the seriousness of the threat. Dughan's response to the report acknowledges thin defenses and the danger to Goldshire. [Chain context](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/A_Fishy_Peril_quest_chain), [Thomas's report: description and Dughan's completion](https://classic.warcraftdb.com/data/quest/39).
- [Further Concerns](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Further_Concerns) includes modern transport dialogue. The mount offer is not treated as evidence of Classic mechanics. The underlying instruction to consult Thomas is supported by the Classic chain.
- The Classic quest inventory also connects Dughan to Northshire's report, Deputy Rainer at Westbrook, the Collector investigation, Hogger's bounty and Westfall. Those relationships were checked as context, rather than turned into invented shared adventures. [Quest list and NPC identity](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/npc=240/marshal-dughan).

Not every individual link's full text was retrievable: several direct Warcraft DB pages returned only an application shell or a cache miss. The entry relies on the accessible scouting texts, report completion and chain synopsis, not invented missing turn-in dialogue. Completion lines are attributed to the actual recipient; Thomas's statements are not written as Dughan's quotations.

The lore does not declare Rolf and Malakai dead for every player, resolve the Collector or Hogger quest, or assume that a reader completed a chain. The private concern about people returning is an original extension of his responsibilities, not a claim that those quests have happened already.

## Forever additions

**Book Return (91775)** sends the player after Paxton's stolen books and a potentially dangerous elemental manual. Dughan provides a bag and recommends company for the mine. **Rare Books (91777)** continues the recovery in Jasperlode and sends the books to Brother Paxton. Both descriptions reinforce his practical concern for scouts facing unknown danger.

- [Book Return text, client-cache source identified as build 1.60.1.70862](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/quest/91775/).
- [Rare Books description and recipient](https://forever.warcraftdb.com/data/quest/91777).
- [Rare Books text with client build attribution](https://bindonforever.com/quest/91777).
- [Book recovery chain outline](https://hyjal.cc/quest/91777/rare-books): Northshire's missing books lead through Kelsey Fargo, McBride and tracking the kobolds, then Dughan's recovery quests.

The book reference in his companion chatter is original dialogue grounded in these quests. No extra relationship with Paxton, magical ability, new quest reward or completion event is invented. Exact rewards, race restrictions and coordinates differ across community references and are not reproduced in the entry.

## Other lore and exclusions

[Dughan's biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Marshal_Dughan) distinguishes original-game material from *Stormrage*, *Dark Riders*, Cataclysm events and noncanonical RPG details. Its later nightmare and the Karlain/Mardigan incident are not assigned to this Classic-era presentation. The RPG's elected-ruler claim is not used. Neither novel nor comic was directly inspected; their summaries are continuity checks, not dialogue sources.

## BuddyDex fiction

The crowded patrol map, private safe-return list, gossip, preferences, SALUTE favorite and 15 companion lines are original additions. The hook is a marshal who sounds occupied with reports but privately cares most about the people who come back. This extends the quest voice without inventing an origin, promotion history, relatives or cowardice.

The secret explains the concern behind the paperwork; the Best Friend message sets it aside for a personal conversation. All 15 personal lines are recorded with Eleven v3 in the existing custom BuddyDex Human Male (Steady) voice, under voice/human-male/lore240_*.ogg.
