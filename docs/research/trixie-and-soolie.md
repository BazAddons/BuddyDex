# Trixie Quikswitch and Soolie Berryfizz

Researched November 8, 2026. Installed Scout records **Trixie Quikswitch 11029**, female (sex 3), level 31, Expert Engineer, and **Soolie Berryfizz 5178**, female (sex 3), level 30, Alchemy Supplies. Both are named Alliance Humanoids, reaction 5, no attackable flag, in Tinker Town, Ironforge. Classic displays **10722** and **3125** route to the established female gnome voice. Trixie qualifies through training/conversation/emotes; Soolie is a **supply vendor**, not the nearby alchemy trainer, and qualifies through normal merchant/conversation/emote interactions.

[Classic identity](https://classicdb.ch/?npc=11029) and its embedded related lists establish her training role and have no direct personal quest starts or ends. [Classic reported gossip](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/npc=11029/trixie-quikswitch) also has her send beginners to her sister Jemma before further instruction. This establishes the sister relationship through reported game dialogue, not just their surname. It does not establish an age order, feud, or the unrelated romantic speculation in later-version user comments. The baster is still a plan; neither a built machine nor a giant turkey is asserted. Her later apprentice title and patch 2.3 training change are excluded in favor of the locally observed Expert Engineer title.

[Forever reference trainer list](https://forevermastery.com/en/npcs/11029) corroborates the profession but labels its spell offerings Classic reference. Exact current skill caps, costs and taught recipes are not claimed. Targeted Forever searches and the installed SpokenQuestsAudioAlliance/Shared/Gossip quest-NPC lookups did not establish a new direct quest for 11029. Jemma's Forever archive quest is researched in [the preceding notes](jemma-and-springspindle.md); it is not reassigned to Trixie. No separate personal book/comic narrative was found in targeted searches.

## Soolie

[Biography](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Soolie_Berryfizz) places her in Berryfizz's Potions and Mixed Drinks. Tally is listed as a relative, but the profile does not invent a precise family relationship or transfer Tally's teaching role to Soolie. It adds location/occupation evidence, not a personal backstory.

[Forever reported recipe inventory](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/npcs/5178/?inventory=13478) and [player-reported vendor stock](https://ahledger.com/wow-forever/npcs/5178-soolie-berryfizz) support that role without establishing present stock, exact prices or restock timers. A search found Soolie as one of many possible empty-vial vendors for [My First Alchemy Set 92109](https://forg.gg/en/forever/quest/92109). A generic ingredient source is not a personal quest role: the quest belongs to the child in Moonbrook. No mentoring relationship, named customer or meeting with that child is invented. Targeted searches and the installed quest-NPC mappings did not find a direct chain to research for 5178. No personal novel/comic story was established. These are bounded research results, not assertions that no undiscovered content exists.
