# BuddyDex v002

## More friends across Azeroth

- Expand the collection to **278 completed NPC profiles**, with personalities, lore, favorite emotes, friendship secrets and personal companion dialogue.
- Build starter-area coverage across both factions, including Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal Glades, Teldrassil, Dun Morogh and Elwynn Forest.
- Add five shared Zephras Isle Buddies accessible to both Skyborne faction paths: Ailee Farheart, Rorian the Dayseeker, Nyalah Brightfire, Coriella Calmbreeze and Zerril Softbreeze.
- Add Windshaper Boro and Aarnor Galestrike, each with a full profile and four signature recordings for Horde players, grounded in the complete earth- and fire-totem initiation chains.
- Research existing lore and quest dialogue before writing each profile, including Forever-specific additions.

## Voices and companions

- Bundle the recorded voices inside BuddyDex, with no separate voice addon required.
- Add character-specific signature recordings using the selected racial voices and Eleven v4 emotional direction.
- Expand voice support across Alliance and Horde races, including Forsaken, orcs, trolls, Tauren and Skyborne.

## Friendship and presentation

- Keep friendship rosters, progress and addon settings specific to each character.
- Befriend named NPCs through supported friendly emotes, including named residents who have no dialogue window.
- Exclude generic role NPCs and children from friendship and companion selection.
- Add BuddyDex emotes to NPC right-click menus with reliable targeting.
- Improve friendship toasts with NPC portraits, clearer progress bars and subdued circular portrait frames.
- Notify chat when Scout discovers a new eligible Buddy candidate.
